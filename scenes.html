Scenes

things I film.

I am a memory hoarder because I am quietly terrified of forgetting. Growing up with so few photos of my favorite times left me with a deep-seated need to archive the present, as if I am making up for every moment that already slipped away. I document everything to relive those days later and to share a glimpse of my world with others—shoutout to my brother for subsidizing my 2TB digital hoarding habit. Because I am usually the one filming, I mostly show up in the occasional selfie, but I am learning how to find a balance where I can be a part of the memories I am so busy saving.

Looking back, many of these videos feel rough and unfinished. The quality is a bit mid and the storytelling isn't quite there yet, but I see them as markers of growth rather than polished pieces. I am just glad I hit record anyway. Those messy clips are the blueprints for the work I am doing now.

I am currently focused on the craft. I have a mountain of footage and a million ideas, but translating a feeling into a finished edit is a massive technical skill. Turning hours of raw clips into something intentional takes time, and I am still figuring out the right pacing and layouts to do justice to these moments. It is a steep learning curve, but it is only upwards and onwards from here. This page holds what I have so far and the space for what comes next.

Exploring San Francisco

Travel • Latest

After wrapping up my exams, I had a brief 5-day break before heading to Norway for a winter vacation with my family. During this time, I flew from Michigan to San Francisco to spend time with my brother and explore the city. The first few days were pretty laid back, just cooking at home, watching TV, and hanging out with my brother and his friends. However, the day before our flight to Norway, I decided to venture out and explore the city on my own. Despite initial hesitations, I ended up taking a 2-hour walk, going through Embarcadero and Ghirardelli Square. Looking back, I wish I had done it sooner to experience more of what the city has to offer.

Jhingura 2015: The Magic Shoe

Comedy • 2015

It was 2015 when I was first introduced to this masterpiece of a video - "Sadma: The Magic Broom". I remember I was so obsessed with it that I made all my friends watch it. Inspired by that video, my friends and I decided to re-create it, but with our twist. We chose to hero a shoe and named it Jhingura. In 2015, none of us had smartphones, so we used a digital camera to record all the scenes on my friend's terrace. Even after seven years, I look back to this video and re-live these moments. One of the best times of my life, and I would highly encourage all of you to do stupid and fun things like this so you all can look back at it and enjoy every bit of it!

Weekend Getaway to Jawhar

Travel • 2021

Another year had passed and we were still confined to our homes. The cancellation of our IGCSE boards caused a prolonged summer vacation, which left me with ample free time. A week before this trip, my dad informed me that we, along with a few other families, were going to Jawhar. Initially, I thought this was going to be just another trip with no fun but it was the perfect getaway. I had tons of fun, from experiencing times of my parents' childhood to listening to interesting stories from everyone present there. Part of it made me realize how much I miss school. We happened to pass my school on the way, and visiting the village there brought back one of my favorite high school memories - the IAYP trip.

Celebrating Stranger Things Day

Celebration • 2020

On November 6th, 2020, amid an ongoing pandemic, I chose to step out and celebrate Stranger Things Day, the day Will Byers goes missing. I was only twelve when I watched Stranger Things. As a tween, it was just a show where a group of children fight monsters to save their friend. However, as I grew older, I began to appreciate the slow and deep character development of each character. Additionally, the show relies heavily on the exploration of relationships such as parenthood, self-love and especially friendship.
